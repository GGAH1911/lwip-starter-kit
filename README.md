# 🌌 LWIP: Starter Kit

> **LLM-Wiki Implementation Protocol (v1.2)**
> "Turning scattered information into a compounding, traceable codebase of knowledge while maintaining zero upkeep cost."

Welcome to your new **Zero-Entropy Knowledge Network**. This template provides the absolute minimum scaffolding required to transform any directory into a self-healing, AI-managed knowledge base.

---

## 💡 Why LWIP?

In traditional knowledge management, information **decays**. Most notes, chat histories, and project documents eventually become a "junk drawer"—scattered, unsearchable, and disconnected. Even with modern AI, most systems treat documents as static chunks to be retrieved (RAG), forcing the AI to rediscover context from scratch every time.

**LWIP reflects a different philosophy:**
- **Knowledge as Code**: We treat knowledge not as a pile of text, but as a living codebase that must be compiled, linked, and refactored.
- **The AI as a Gardener**: Instead of just searching, the AI is hired to actively maintain the "structural integrity" of your thoughts.
- **Compounding Returns**: Because every node is interconnected and traceable, the wiki gets exponentially more useful as it grows. 

By enforcing **Structural Zero-Entropy**, we ensure that your knowledge base remains a sharp, high-fidelity asset that never enters the stage of digital rot.

## 🏛️ Heritage & Evolution (The 3-Tier Lineage)

LWIP is the result of a rigorous three-stage evolution in agentic knowledge management:

1. **The Seed (Andrej Karpathy)**: 
   Karpathy's original "LLM-Wiki" essay proposed the fundamental metaphor: The LLM as the Librarian, the Wiki as the Codebase, and Obsidian as the IDE. It introduced the core idea of compounding knowledge over stateless retrieval.

2. **The Evolution (Engineering Core)**: 
   While the original concept was a powerful vision, it lacked formal quantitative standards and automated lifecycle management. LWIP bridges this gap by introducing the "Symbiosis" framework and the mandate for **Structural Zero-Entropy**—transforming a conceptual library into a structurally consistent, alert-driven knowledge mesh.

3. **The Standardization (LWIP v1.2)**: 
   The final stage is the implementation of a universal, agent-agnostic engineering protocol. LWIP introduces **JIT Tooling** (Agent-generated maintenance scripts) and **Standardized Lifecycle Gates** to ensure the system is portable across any project and any LLM.

## 🚀 Quick Start (For the Human)

This template is designed to be as "plug-and-play" as possible. There are no dependencies to install, no servers to boot, and no complex configurations.

1. **Extract** this kit into the root of your project directory (or create a new empty directory for a personal Wiki).
2. **Open your LLM Agent** (e.g., Cursor, Claude, Codex, ChatGPT).
3. **Point the Agent** to the `agent.md` file and give it the prompt: 
   *"Please read `agent.md` and adopt your role."*

That's it. Your Agent is now the **Knowledge Librarian** of this project.

---

## 📖 How it Works

The magic of LWIP isn't in complex code; it's in the **Governance**. 

*   **`agent.md` (The Constitution):** Forces the AI to adopt a specific persona, bound by strict mathematical rules (`0-Gap`, `100%-Lineage`). The AI will no longer write forgettable chat answers; it will write permanent, interconnected documentation.
*   **`lifecycle.md` (The Engine):** Instructs the AI on *how* to operate. Before you finish a session, the AI must automatically generate temporary scripts to audit the knowledge graph and fix any broken links.
*   **`docs/index.md` (The Dashboard):** A single-glance health monitor showing page count, orphans, and suggested next actions.
*   **`docs/log.md` (The Timeline):** An append-only chronological record of every operation, ensuring no fact is ever truly lost.

## ⚙️ The 5 Pillars of Operation

Once initialized, your AI Librarian follows a standard operational cycle to maintain system integrity:

1.  **Ingest**: Absorbing new raw data while maintaining systematic **100%-Lineage** to source files.
2.  **Query & Promote**: Substantive Q&A answers are automatically promoted into permanent Wiki pages.
3.  **Consolidate**: Merging overlapping pages and organizing links into structured **Role-Based Tables**.
4.  **Prune**: Safely archiving stale content with a logged impact assessment.
5.  **Audit**: Running a **Zero-Entropy Scan** before every session closure to ensure 0 broken links.
